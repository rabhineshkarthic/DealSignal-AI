# Investment Committee Memorandum

## Transaction Overview
This memorandum evaluates the proposed transaction in which Accenture plc ("acquirer") acquires EPAM Systems, Inc. ("target"). DEAL DATA reports that Accenture plc has revenue of 69672.977, EBITDA of 11594.049, net_income of 7678.433, cash of 10165.245, and debt of 5142.265, with an acquirer share basis of 612.213453, defined in DEAL DATA as weighted-average basic shares used as a modelling proxy, and a share price of 198.9. EPAM Systems, Inc. reports revenue of 5457.056, EBITDA of 644.814, net_income of 377.678, free_cash_flow of 612.691, cash of 789.397, and debt of 25.0.

The supplied offer terms consist of an offer_enterprise_value of 6300.0. The consideration structure comprises a cash_percentage of 0.7 and a stock_percentage of 0.3. As noted in DETERMINISTIC CALCULATIONS, debt funding of the cash portion is a modelling convention of the deterministic model, not a confirmed financing plan.

## Executive Summary
The deal team has reviewed the supplied DEAL DATA and DETERMINISTIC CALCULATIONS. The strategic rationale remains unassessed due to the absence of product, technology, capability, and customer data in DEAL DATA. Valuation methods produce an enterprise value range from a low of 5287.4748 under the Comparable Companies method to a high of 9863.0608 under the Discounted Cash Flow method, with the offer_enterprise_value of 6300.0 sitting within the range. Synergy assumptions (annual_cost_synergy of 250.0 and annual_revenue_synergy_ebitda of 75.0) yield a deterministic pv_synergies of 908.6724 and a net_synergy_value of 608.6724 after deducting an integration_cost of 300.0, though these synergy assumptions remain unverified. Financial, commercial, legal, and operational due diligence is severely constrained by extensive information gaps, including the complete absence of multi-period financial statements, material contracts, customer concentration data, organizational charts, and committed financing term sheets. Major unresolved issues include unverified synergy assumptions, unconfirmed financing arrangements, and pervasive information gaps across all diligence workstreams.

## Strategic Rationale
The Deal Lead and Strategic Fit Analyst observe that DEAL DATA reports revenue of 69672.977 and EBITDA of 11594.049 for Accenture plc, and revenue of 5457.056 and EBITDA of 644.814 for EPAM Systems, Inc. Operational breakdowns and commercial evidence to validate these synergy assumptions are absent.

Pro forma shares outstanding are reported as 622.8687, with seller ownership of 0.0171 and existing acquirer holder ownership of 0.9829.

Per the financing convention in the deterministic model, the cash portion of the equity purchase price is modelled as funded entirely with new acquisition debt of 4945.0779 at the new_debt_interest_rate of 0.055, generating an annual pre-tax interest expense of 271.9793 and an annual after-tax interest expense of 203.1685. Financing sources, debt term sheets, refinancing terms for target existing debt, rollover equity terms, and purchase-price adjustment mechanisms are absent.

## Key Risks & Unresolved Matters
Evidence-backed risks include the wide enterprise value dispersion across valuation methods (ranging from 5287.4748 to 9863.0608) and the substantial debt obligations introduced by the financing modelling convention (4945.0779 of new debt generating annual after-tax interest expense of 203.1685). Unverified assumptions include all valuation inputs (comparable multiples, precedent multiples, FCF growth rate, WACC, terminal growth rate), synergy assumptions (cost synergies, revenue synergies, realization years, integration cost, tax rate, discount rate), and transaction assumptions (cash/stock percentages, interest rate). Extensive information gaps persist across product fit, financial statements, market data, legal contracts, operational systems, and committed financing.

## Conditions / Required Next Steps
Before any transaction commitment can be considered, the following diligence actions and conditions must be fulfilled:
- Obtain detailed product, technology, and capability disclosures for EPAM Systems, Inc. to evaluate strategic fit.
- Obtain multi-period financial statements and perform a comprehensive quality of earnings review.
- Acquire customer concentration reports, key commercial contracts, and customer retention data.
- Conduct legal due diligence on material contracts, change-of-control provisions, litigation records, IP portfolios, and regulatory compliance.
- Obtain departmental headcount data, organizational charts, IT infrastructure inventories, and real estate lease agreements to test synergy feasibility.
- Obtain committed financing term sheets and definitive debt financing agreements to replace the deterministic financing convention.
