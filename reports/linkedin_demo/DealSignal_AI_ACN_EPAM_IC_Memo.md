## Key Risks & Unresolved Matters
The Risk Analyst identified evidence-backed risks, including valuation positioning relative to market methods, synergy execution timing risk (arising from full run-rate synergy recognition in year one without a ramp-up), and financing leverage reliance under the deterministic financing convention. Unverified assumptions include comparable and precedent EV/EBITDA multiples, DCF forecast parameters, cost and revenue synergy assumptions, new debt interest rate, and consideration proportions. Extensive information gaps exist across product, customer, financial, commercial, legal, operational, and financing dimensions, as noted by multiple workstream agents and the IC Review Context.

## Conditions / Required Next Steps
Prior to any investment commitment, the deal team must:
- Obtain multi-period historical financial statements for EPAM Systems, Inc.
- Obtain detailed customer contracts, customer lists, and retention metrics to evaluate customer concentration risk.
- Verify the operational feasibility of the annual_cost_synergy of 250.0 and annual_revenue_synergy_ebitda of 75.0 through departmental headcount, organizational, and procurement reviews.
- Audit historical integration expenditures to evaluate the adequacy of the integration_cost of 300.0.
- Obtain formal financing commitments and term sheets to replace the deterministic model's financing convention.
- Conduct comprehensive legal, tax, litigation, and intellectual property audits.
